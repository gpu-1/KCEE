# Engineering validation
Demo equations: sigma = 6 F L / (b t²); delta = 4 F L³ / (E b t³). Assumes an ideal slender cantilever with rectangular cross-section and load at the free end. The L-root joint is not analyzed. A factor-of-safety threshold here is only a screening constraint and cannot establish that a bracket is safe.

Required next gates: certified material allowables, dimensional units, bolt/contact loads, fillet/stress concentration resolution, mesh convergence, equilibrium and energy checks, comparison with an independent analytical benchmark, physical testing and documented reviewer approval. Thermal/CFD need domain-specific benchmarks. AI output is not a solver or validation authority.
